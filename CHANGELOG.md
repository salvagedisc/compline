# Changelog

Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/fr/1.1.0/). Versions: `0.x` prototype, `1.0.0` launch of the permanent world.

## [Unreleased]

No version has been published yet: everything below will make up `0.1.0`.

No Man's Land left the pack on 2026-11-13 ([0019](docs/decisions/0019-leaving-no-mans-land.md)). The older entries below that mention it, the Friendly Moon, its fauna or its music record what the pack contained before that date; the last entries of each section and the Removed section say what changed.

### Added
- Brumaire, snow, sand and ice: in a snowy biome, snow on the tops of trees and ledges, held by the bright needles with a ragged band down the sides of the leaves; snow that keeps its grain in the sun and glitters; sand that stays warm in the sun and glitters; clear ice mirrored like water, packed ice like a tarnished mirror; the air trembling over the horizon of a hot desert, with less mist near the player. The `Pluie` settings page becomes `Pluie, neige, chaleur`.
- Brumaire, rain: thin light-grey drops instead of the game's blue streaks; ground that darkens and shines under rain and dries slowly afterwards; puddles on flat ground that mirror the landscape and ripple under the drops; rain curtains over the distance; a bright lightning bolt that flashes the whole landscape; a warmer grey under rain. New `Pluie` settings page.
- **A house Warden and a house moose** (`compline` 0.18.0, `faune` module, client): both redrawn and reanimated with GeckoLib in the spirit of Archie's Dinosaurs, with blends between animations and a shared house layer (pace matched to the real speed, soft head turn, lean in turns, swinging tendrils and dewlap). The Warden plays its ten house animations at the game's moments (emerge, dig, sniff, roar, attack, sonic boom, listen) and its glow beats with its heart; the Alex's Mobs moose keeps its AI and only changes look (antlers that fall, snow, calf). `faune.enabled = false` in `compline-client.toml` gives back the original models at once.
- **A zoo in Archie's guidebook** (`compline` 0.17.0, `zoo` module): every creature of the Overworld gets four stamps, Spot, Photograph (with an Exposure camera), Tame or house, Master (the other stamps and a minute of watching), and none asks for a kill. A green bookmark opens the zoo in the guidebook: a welcome spread, then the species of each habitat drawn in 3D, an ink silhouette until spotted. J opens the guidebook without holding it. Archie's Obsidiceratops and Chorusoatl pages no longer send players to the Nether or the End.
- `compline` 0.16.0, `vent` module: the pack's own wind (direction that turns slowly, strength from the weather and the altitude, gust fronts), the same for every player of a server; campfire smoke led by that wind, one larger plume over several campfires, bluish over a soul campfire, after Better Smoke ([0032](docs/decisions/0032-house-wind.md)).
- Brumaire: grass and flowers lie down under the steps of the player and of animals; rain and snow slant with the wind.
- Brumaire, water: the bottom shows through the surface, bent by the waves and fading with depth into a grey-green tinted by the biome; banks, trees and clouds are mirrored, more at a low angle, in hard-edged patches along waves drawn on the pixel grid; a sun glint on the ripples; the light reaches the bottom weakened and gathered into moving caustics; from below, the world above shows through Snell's window. New `Eau` settings page (murk, landscape reflections, caustics, pixel waves).
- Brumaire, volumetric clouds: cumulus with flat bases and bright edges, lit by the sun, the moon and the sky, pushed by the wind, thicker and greyer in the rain, glowing at sunset, with their shadows on the ground; they replace the game's clouds under the shader.
- Brumaire, light and air: a physical sky (sunsets, moon by its phase, paler sky in humid biomes) computed once per frame, from which the colours of the sun, the moon and the sky light are read; distant air that blues the far landscape; mist that follows the biome (olive in swamps, bluish in the cold, thin and sandy in deserts) and glows toward a low sun; soft shadows that widen with the distance of the caster; screen occlusion of the sky light; sage greens and a matte grade.
- `compline-ambiance`, water and air: the water seen from inside takes the colour of the biome and absorbs each colour at its own rate, darkens with depth, carries floating specks and shows the sky through a circle at the surface (Snell's window); lakes and the sea reflect the sky with a sun glint; a thin low mist lies on the water and in the low ground at dawn, dusk and in the rain. Three new settings (`Sous l'eau`, `Reflets sur l'eau`, `Brume basse`).
- `compline` 0.14.0: the `relief` module also gives the resource pack the water colour of the biome and the height of the surface above the camera.
- **Cooking by hand: Farmer's Delight replaces Kaleidoscope Cookery** ([0030](docs/decisions/0030-farmers-delight-kitchen.md)). Farmer's Delight 1.21.1-1.3.4 (cooking pot, cutting board, skillet, stove, keg), Hands-On Delight 1.5.0 + Diegesis 1.0.0-beta.10 (its stations used in the world with the hands, stirring stars for extra servings), Create: Central Kitchen 2.6.2b + Create: Dragons Plus 1.11.9 (Create automation of the kitchen). The knives keep going through the Overgeared forge; the Nether salad, the netherite knife and the crimson and warped cabinets leave with the pack sort.
- **Cinematic Interactions** 0.18.30 (jar in `pack/mods/`, MIT, CurseForge only): the vanilla stations (workbench, furnaces, anvil, grindstone, smithing table, stonecutter, enchanting table, brewing stand, loom, cartography table, lectern, campfire, beacon) are used in first-person scenes with the hands; Sneak + use opens the normal window.
