### Fixed
- No Man's Land in French: `npm run check-fr` now also checks the Friendly Moon dialogues, which NML keeps outside the usual language files (it missed them until now; all of them already have a French text). The hard-coded "Successfully linked bell" message is translated by `fallow` 0.6.2 ([translation](docs/plan/traduction.md#what-stays-in-english-or-needs-watching)).
- Pack sort ([details](docs/plan/tri-du-pack.md)): Aeronautics levitite powder made from crushed calcite (it needed end stone); the Farm & Charm mincer no longer triples ores nor gives stone, wood or leather; the Kaleidoscope Compat setting that disables the Farm & Charm cooking pot recipes is shipped; 28 Immersive Enchanting costs rewritten without Nether or End items, Soul Speed disabled; Twigs rhyolite no longer generated; In Control! targeted a cave centipede id that does not exist; the Supplementaries globe no longer shows coordinates, Quark tooltips no longer show food values; Icy's Better Horses Paint, glide harness and Andalusian save disabled; the Cold Sweat hearth and the excellent telescopic lens no longer need Nether materials.
- The Friendly Moon never appeared at the Meeting Point, nor its shadow in the sky after the dream: ATMOSPHERICS gives the sky back to No Man's Land only once the Friendly Moon is visible, which only No Man's Land's own sky can make happen. The `ciel` module of `fallow` 0.4.10 draws them after ATMOSPHERICS' sky until it hands over, and leaves the sky to the moonlight dream ([module](docs/plan/mod-fallow.md#the-friendly-moon-under-atmospherics)).
- With Distant Horizons, the mist of a clearing never closed: far mountains drowned in the mist stayed visible as pale silhouettes against the sky up to the end of the LOD radius. The `horizon` module of `fallow` 0.4.11 dissolves the far landscape into the sky at the end of the mist, capped by the new client option `horizon.ringMaxDistance` (512 blocks), so a mist ring always remains. It also skips Distant Horizons' terrain-to-LOD fade when LODs are not drawn, which could blend a frozen LOD image into the mist ([module](docs/plan/mod-fallow.md#horizon-module)).
- Breaking a vigil fire, a clearing lantern or a charged dissipator left its clearing in place. With `fallow` 0.4.12 the mist comes back over its disc, except where another source still holds it; `/fallow brume <radius>` does the same for operators ([module](docs/plan/mod-fallow.md#clairiere-module)).
- Placing or breaking a clearing source changed the mist and the grass at once. With `fallow` 0.4.12 the clearing spreads ring by ring from the source (and shrinks from the edge when it is removed), with cloud puffs, and the mist thins or thickens over several seconds.
- With most items in hand, the first-person hand went down and up again without end and the item never showed: Punchy! read the stack size Sensible Stackables gives (32) against the item's base one (64) and replayed its equip animation every tick. `fallow` 0.4.13 makes Punchy! ignore the stack size when it compares held items ([fix](docs/plan/mod-fallow.md#punchy-and-sensible-stackables-the-hand-that-kept-jumping)).
- No Man's Land: the scutes offering line never loaded (condition on a non-existent item, `nomansland:tortoise_scute`).
- Seen in the full pack on 2026-11-01, after the first game on `fallow` 0.6.0:
  - large glowing blobs everywhere in daylight: Shine had never been configured and ran at its default bloom (strength 8, threshold 0.15), so every sunlit surface glowed, and the glints on water even more. `config/shine.json` now keeps a light halo around fires and lanterns only (strength 2, threshold 0.75, radius 160, 48 blocks);
  - the rain turned bright yellow when facing the sun and hid the view: `fallow` 0.6.1 drops the gold back-light of the `averse` module, draws the drops in a light grey taken from the mist at 70 % opacity, and the new drop texture has fewer, shorter and fainter streaks;
  - the rain curtains closed the view: they now start at 24 blocks instead of 14 and are half as opaque;
  - the glints on water are dimmer.
- The "you need an axe / a pickaxe" hint of the `outils` module never showed: its timer started at the smallest possible value, so the first subtraction overflowed and the hint always looked too recent. Fixed in `fallow` 0.6.0 (found while writing the `rayons` module, which had the same mistake).
