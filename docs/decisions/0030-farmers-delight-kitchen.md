# 0030 — Farmer's Delight replaces Kaleidoscope Cookery

- **Date**: 2026-11-15
- **Status**: kept (the team's choice of 2026-11-15); starts on a dedicated server, not played. Supersedes the cooking part of [0002](0002-agriculture.md) (A, the condition on Farm & Charm, and F)

## Context
Kaleidoscope Cookery was the pack's cooking since [0002](0002-agriculture.md). Two young mods make cooking a gesture in the world instead of a window: **Hands-On Delight** (the Farmer's Delight cooking pot, cutting board, skillet, stove and keg used by hand, first-person scenes, stirring stars that give extra servings) and **Cinematic Interactions** (the same idea for the vanilla stations: workbench, furnaces, anvil, grindstone, smithing table, stonecutter, enchanting table, brewing stand, loom, cartography table, lectern, campfire, beacon). The team likes both; Hands-On Delight needs Farmer's Delight, which the pack had turned down because Kaleidoscope Cookery already did the cooking.

Kaleidoscope Cookery has no menus (wok, pot, board and steamer are already used in the world), so neither Cinematic Interactions' API nor Hands-On Delight applies to it; only a house port on Diegesis (Hands-On Delight's library) would, at about 5,000 lines of Java for five stations. Nobody has played the pack yet, so no world depends on Kaleidoscope's blocks.

## Options
| Criterion | A. Keep Kaleidoscope Cookery | B. Farmer's Delight + Hands-On Delight |
|---|---|---|
| Cooking by hand | Already in the world, without scenes | Scenes with the hands, stirring, pouring; menus come back if Hands-On Delight leaves |
| Style | Chinese kitchen furniture | Close to vanilla |
| French | Translated by the pack | Farmer's Delight official; Hands-On Delight and Diegesis translated by the pack |
| Create automation | KaleidoscopeCookery-Automation | Create: Central Kitchen |
| Maturity | 1.6.0 | Farmer's Delight mature; Hands-On Delight and Diegesis a few days old |
| Work | None | Swap the mods, adapt three scripts |

## Decision
Option B, with Cinematic Interactions:

- **Removed**: Kaleidoscope Cookery, Kaleidoscope Compat, KaleidoscopeCookery-Automation, their French translations and `config/kaleidoscope_compat.jsonc`.
- **Added**: Farmer's Delight 1.21.1-1.3.4 (MIT), Hands-On Delight 1.5.0 and Diegesis 1.0.0-beta.10 (MIT, Modrinth), Create: Central Kitchen 2.6.2b with its library Create: Dragons Plus 1.11.9, Cinematic Interactions 0.18.30 (MIT). Cinematic Interactions is not on Modrinth: its NeoForge build from CurseForge is committed in `pack/mods/`, like the atlas ports.
- **Scripts**: the forge recipes of the kitchen knives move to Farmer's Delight's iron, golden and diamond knives (`tri_recettes.js`); the Nether items of the pack sort are now the Nether salad, the netherite knife and the crimson and warped cabinets (`tri.js`, both sides); the tannery no longer removes Kaleidoscope's rotten flesh → leather drying recipe, which left with the mod. Farmer's Delight cuts leather armour, horse armour and saddles back into leather: kept, like the other recyclings of existing leather ([tannery](../plan/tannerie.md)).
- **Farm & Charm keeps the farm, not the kitchen** (the team's choice of 2026-11-15): its stove, cooking pot, roaster and crafting bowl leave the game (`tri.js`, both sides). `cuisine.js` copies every recipe of the first three to Farmer's Delight's cooking pot, with the same ingredients, result and container (27 dishes: yeast, teas, goulash, breads, cake, roasts, patties…), and the six crafting bowl recipes (butter, dough, salad, oatmeal, pet foods) to the workbench. Its onion soup is not copied: Farmer's Delight has its own, and Farm & Charm's leaves. Its mincer stays, as a farm machine. Tomato, onion and cabbage/lettuce share the `c:crops/*` tags of both mods, so Farmer's Delight recipes that ask for a tag accept Farm & Charm's crops.
- **Duplicates** (found in game on 2026-11-15): Farmer's Delight's tomato and onion duplicated Farm & Charm's. Farm & Charm's are kept, because they already follow the seasons and Farmer's Delight's recipes ask for `c:crops/tomato` and `c:crops/onion`; Farmer's Delight's tomato, onion, seeds, wild plants, crates and rotten tomato join the pack sort (`tri.js`). Its wild tomatoes, onions, carrots, potatoes and beetroots no longer generate (`kubejs/data/farmersdelight/neoforge/biome_modifier/`, Farm & Charm has the same wild plants). Its cabbage and rice stay (no equivalent) and now follow the seasons: cabbage in spring and autumn like the lettuce, rice in summer and autumn. Farmer's Delight's cutting board does not cut tomatoes or onions: they go whole into the pot. Three ropes remain (Farmer's Delight, Farm & Charm, Supplementaries).
- **Everything by hand** (asked by the team on 2026-11-15): Hands-On Delight's `remove_crafting_recipes=true` (`config/handson_delight.properties`) moves Farmer's Delight's 95 food crafts (sandwiches, salads, rolls, pies, feasts) from the workbench to the cutting board; the `handson_delight:assembled` tag adds other mods' dishes (cookie, pumpkin pie, Farm & Charm's breakfast, salad, oatmeal, dough and pet foods, Alex's Mobs' burger and soup, the coral stew, Longwings' brews). Bread is no longer three wheat at the workbench: the dough is kneaded on the board, then baked in the furnace, the smoker, on the campfire, in the skillet or on the stove (`cuisine.js` adds the campfire recipe, which the skillet and stove use). Beetroot soup and mushroom stew are cooked in the pot only; the hamsters' cucumber is sliced with a knife on the board; the five foods that only cooked in the smoker also cook on the campfire. Left at the workbench: golden apple and carrot, crates and bags, Companions' big bread (a summoning item), the hamsters' cheese. Not checked in game: whether the board accepts every tagged dish (it logs a line when it cannot lay one; none at start-up).
- **Final check** (asked by the team on 2026-11-15): every item of the pack compared by English name across mods, and every food recipe outside the hand stations. `fusions.js` merges 22 duplicated items into the one kept, in recipes (208, rebuilt under `fallow:fusion/`), loot and the tags Farm & Charm's recipes ask for; the merged items are hidden from EMI (`client_scripts/fusions.js`). Kitchen: Farm & Charm's bacon, minced beef, chicken parts, lamb ham, pasta, dough and dog food give way to Farmer's Delight's (the board makes them; Farm & Charm's mincer now gives them too), its flour and Create's dough to Create's wheat flour and Farmer's Delight's dough, Naturalist's cooked egg to the fried egg, Naturalist's clam and crab meat (its crab is cut) to Hybrid Aquatic's. Tannery: Farmer's Delight's tree bark and Naturalist's fat become Tannery's, so the board and Create's saw feed the tanning barrel. Others: one rope (Supplementaries', in flax: Farmer's Delight's and Farm & Charm's straw ropes leave), Create's copper nugget, Spelunkery's diamond shard and glowstick, Hybrid Aquatic's shark tooth. Farm & Charm's bags of vanilla crops leave for Farmer's Delight's crates. Hybrid Aquatic's butchering (fish, crab and lobster claws) moves from the workbench to the knife on the board; Regions Unexplored's barley no longer bakes into bread; chicken feed and the hamsters' food mix join the board. Farm & Charm's cooked cod, cooked salmon and roasted chicken are renamed in French (« Morue au beurre et aux légumes », « Saumon au beurre et au maïs », « Poulet rôti aux pâtes ») so they no longer read like the vanilla items. Left as they are: Quark's stone variants next to Create's and Twigs' (building, not cooking), the three cattails and two duckweeds of the fauna mods, the two pearls and two acorns (their mods use them in code). Hands-On Delight now lists 92 Farmer's Delight crafts on the board: organic compost and the rope fence and gate, rebuilt by the merge, are no longer counted, which keeps them at the workbench.
- **French**: Cinematic Interactions (528 texts), Create: Central Kitchen (63), Hands-On Delight (26) and Diegesis (3) are translated in `pack/kubejs/assets/`.

To judge in game: the extra servings of the stirring stars (no setting), Cinematic Interactions next to Overgeared's anvil and Punchy (its Punchy conflict is fixed since 0.18.1), Hands-On Delight on a Sable ship.

## Rejected
- House Kaleidoscope port on Diegesis: a code project of its own for the same result.
- Keeping both cooking mods: two pots, two boards, two sets of knives.
