# 0011 — Terrain and far view: Tectonic, Streams Reflowing, Distant Horizons

- **Date**: 2026-10-25
- **Status**: kept (the team's choice); server start validated without a player, nothing seen in game yet; its "on NML" part is superseded by [0019](0019-leaving-no-mans-land.md) (Distant Horizons and the `horizon` module stay)
- **Supersedes**: [0001](0001-nml-vs-terralith.md) for the terrain, and the "Distant Horizons pending" part of [0007](0007-ambiance-performance.md)

## Context
The team wants a more spectacular relief and real rivers on top of No Man's Land, and wants to admire the map wherever the mist has been lifted, without losing the mist everywhere else. Research of 2026-10-25: jars of Tectonic 3.0.28, Streams Reflowing 2.15.2, Distant Horizons 3.3.3, NML 1.6.1, Monoliths 1.5.2, Biolith, Lithostitched, Sable and ATMOSPHERICS opened (`javap`), issue trackers read, then the full pack started on a dedicated test server.

## Decision

**Tectonic + Streams Reflowing, with No Man's Land.** They do not compete: NML places its biomes through Biolith and ships no terrain noise; Tectonic replaces the overworld `noise_settings` and density functions (no other jar or datapack of the pack touches them); Streams carves its streams as a feature and has explicit NML, Tectonic, Create, Sable and Distant Horizons support.
- `pack/config/tectonic.json`: generated by Tectonic, only `river_lanterns = false` (no artificial light in the world). Height stays -64 to 320: more would break the clearing conversion (`/fillbiome` is capped at 32,768 blocks), weigh on Distant Horizons and Chunky, and must never change once the world exists (Tectonic #540).
- `pack/config/streamsreflowing.properties`: preset "large scale, light" (`watershedSize = BLOCKS_1536`, `terrainAccuracyLevel = LOW`, `minStreamLength = 150`, `lakeMinArea = 1500`) and `growDensity = 1.0` for an emptier world. These keys are frozen when the world is created. `spillOnStreamsOnly` stays true (Create tanks do not overflow), `offloadRegionBuilds` stays false (the seed is not sent to clients).
- `pack/config/streamsreflowing-client.properties`: stream particles scanned over 128 blocks (400 per tick) instead of 256 (1000), the mist hides the rest.
- `pack/kubejs/data/streamsreflowing/tags/worldgen/structure/always_generates.json`: the six Monoliths monuments keep their footprint, water moves aside.
- Lithostitched stays mandatory: Tectonic uses it but does not declare it (Tectonic #534).

**Distant Horizons, driven by the mist.** DH draws its LODs separately and fogs them with its own fog, measured as a fraction of the LOD radius; it knows nothing about ATMOSPHERICS, and by default it even erases the vanilla fog.
- `pack/config/DistantHorizons.toml` (file generated by DH 3.3.3): `enableVanillaFog = true` (the ATMOSPHERICS mist stays), far fog `farFogMin = farFogMax = 1` (LODs fully hidden when nothing drives them), LOD radius 96 chunks, 3 threads at 50 %, DH clouds and DH's takeover of vanilla graphics settings off (Better Clouds), auto-updater off, real-time updates over 96 chunks on the server.
- New `horizon` module of the `compline` mod 0.3.0 ([mod-compline](../plan/mod-compline.md#horizon-module)): every frame, the terrain mist is copied into DH's far fog, linear, thickness 0 to 1. Where the mist ends before the edge of the normal terrain, the LODs are not drawn at all; where it is lifted, the landscape appears with the same curve as nearby terrain.
- Clearing profiles of the ATMOSPHERICS preset (`compline_v3.json`): mist 40 → 500 blocks by day, 16 → 260 at night (instead of 28 → 124 and 8 → 84). With 124, below the 128 blocks of terrain, no LOD could ever be seen.
- Two bugs of DH 3.3.3 fixed by the `horizon` module: the dedicated server (and single player) crashed when creating the world because DH binds to Chunky before Chunky has started ("Chunky is not loaded", observed on the test server); and DH's read of `GL_POLYGON_MODE` overflows its native memory on Apple Silicon Macs (DH #1346, random JVM crashes, fixed upstream after 3.3.3).

## Rejected or postponed
- Increased height (Tectonic `max_y` 640): see above.
- Separate Sable Render Distance: requires 1.8.7 (beta) with DH 3.3; to evaluate with an airship in game.
- `chunkGeneratorMode = INTERNAL_SERVER` for DH: kept on the default `FEATURES`; to switch if LODs do not match the real Tectonic terrain.

## Conditions and tests
In-game tests in [mod-compline](../plan/mod-compline.md#in-game-tests): H1 to H12 (far view) and W1 to W10 (terrain). Everything that is frozen at world creation (Tectonic, Streams) must be validated before the Chunky pregeneration of the permanent world.
