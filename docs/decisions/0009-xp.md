# 0009 — Removal of experience

- **Date**: 2026-10-19
- **Status**: kept (team choice); validated on the server side, not yet tested with a player

## Context
Wayfarer, the No Man's Land team's modpack, is played without XP ([research](../archive/plan/recherche/2026-10-19/wayfarer.md), idea 5). In Fallow, enchanting is already paid for in materials ([0008](0008-enchantement.md)): XP is now just a gauge and an invisible currency that tells no story. The team asked for a study before deciding, then chose to remove it. Full study: [xp.md](../plan/xp.md).

## Options
| Criterion | Keep XP | Remove XP |
|---|---|---|
| Remaining uses | Anvil, copying and transmutation in Immersive Enchanting, Envelope's address | All four are replaced by materials or a config |
| What the player loses | Nothing | Small bonuses: blue moon ×2, lapis bag, Create nuggets, 5 XP per Wildex species |
| Technical cost | None | An anvil mixin (`fallow` mod), a KubeJS script, three configs, Clumps removed |
| Tone | A green bar that rises for no reason | Progress comes from the world: items found, lore, blessings |

## Decision
**Remove XP in a single version**:
- `xp` module of the `fallow` mod 0.2.0: the anvil no longer asks for levels, its "Trop cher !" (too expensive) limit becomes wear ("Usure : n", "Trop usé !"); the XP bar is hidden, the horse jump gauge stays;
- `pack/kubejs/server_scripts/sans_xp.js`: no orbs, no point gains; experience bottles removed from loot; experience nugget removed from Create's crushing recipes (29 in this pack) and experience block left without a recipe;
- Immersive Enchanting: copying an ancient book costs a book and a feather, transmuting it 4 amethyst shards (starting value);
- Envelope: naming a mailbox no longer costs anything; Exposure, Wildex and the lapis bag no longer give XP;
- Clumps removed from the pack.

Defaults chosen: merging enchanted items at the anvil remains allowed (`blockEnchantMerge = false` in `[xp]`); the module is turned off with `enabled = false`.

## Rejected
- Modrinth mods that remove XP: none fixes the anvil, none is translated.
- Keeping a reduced XP: a half-measure that keeps the bar and the invisible currency.
