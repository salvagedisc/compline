# The `fallow-interface` resource pack

Fallow's interface: vanilla screens, the screens of the mods, the HUD and the Questlog journal, in one style. It replaced Dark Coffee GUI and Modded Coffee GUI on 2026-10-26 (all rights reserved, and their author does not answer).

## Sources

The maintainer asked to start from an existing pack that may be modified, then adjust it to the pack's mods. After comparing about thirty interface packs for 1.21.1, the chosen mix is:

| Pack | Author | Licence | What it brings |
|---|---|---|---|
| GUI Revision 2.2.0 | Vixel | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | Vanilla screens and HUD |
| GUI Revision Compats 43.0 | Fyoncle | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | Screens of Supplementaries, Amendments, Quark, Cold Sweat, Curios, EMI, JEI, Exposure, Farm & Charm, Immersive Enchanting, Overgeared, Chipped, Naturalist, Entity Texture Features |
| Hollow GUI 2 | Melee | CC BY 4.0 | The two curls at the top of the panels |
| Questlog | Infernal Studios | Apache-2.0 | The journal's layout (redrawn) |
| Minecraft 1.21.1 client | Mojang | Minecraft EULA | The crafter's screen and the widgets GUI Revision lacks |

Rejected: Overgrown Flowery GUI (its licence forbids publishing a modified version), STONEBORN and Whimscape (all rights reserved), CozyUI+ (GPL-3, but its high-definition look feels like an app rather than Minecraft).

Licence of the result: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, like GUI Revision. Fallow must therefore not be used commercially (no paid access, no revenue sharing on the pack, monetisation off on its Modrinth page). The recoloured textures of other mods stay under their own licences, listed in `pack/resourcepacks/fallow-interface/CREDITS.md`; only mods whose licence allows a modified copy to be shared are recoloured, so Adorable Hamster Pets, Critters and Companions, Mowzie's Mobs and No Man's Land (all rights reserved) keep their own screens.

## Build

`scripts/interface/build.py` (Python 3 + Pillow 12.1 or newer, authoring tool, not run by CI) downloads the pinned releases and the game's client (checked by SHA-1), keeps only the textures the game or a mod of the pack reads (compat files made for other versions of a mod, OptiFine's and the files left as they were are dropped, and a texture keeps the nine-slice metadata of the one it replaces), and writes the pack and its icon. It stops if a texture named in one of its coordinate tables is no longer found. Run `npm run check-fr` once before, so that the mod jars are in `.cache/jars`. Then `packwiz refresh` in `pack/`.

What the script changes:

- **Colours.** GUI Revision uses a handful of greys; each one maps to Fallow's palette: mist panels `#CBCAB6`, sage slots `#828C7A`, bone highlights `#E8E5D0`, moss darks and outline `#1A1F1C`.
- **Screens in other colours.** GUI Revision paints a few vanilla screens in wood or purple (crafting table, loom, cartography table, enchanting table, shulker box); they are first brought back to its greys, so that they take the same palette. The same goes for Chipped's workbench and Quark's backpack, whose leather pocket becomes a darker mist pocket with bone stitches.
- **Slots.** Every slot (vanilla, compats, recoloured mods, the hotbar) is redrawn over its whole cell as a recessed well with rounded corners: an outline a step darker than the well, darker at the top and left and fading towards the ends, a soft two-row shadow under the top edge, and a lighter edge along the bottom and right. There is no light line on top and no near-black edge, and no slot is round. A placeholder drawn inside a slot (the loom's banner and dye, Overgeared's blueprint) is kept. The wells where the player and the mounts stand (and the black ones of mod screens) are deeper, filled edge to edge with a fine two-tone grain, without the inner shadow. Each cut corner takes the colour beside it (a frame, a section border, transparency), so that only where four slots meet does a small cross of panel show, the same on every screen. Marks of the source's old slot art left within two pixels of a slot (light corners, a line along the player's well) are cleared. Every block of 2x2 to 3x3 slots with room around it (the inventory's crafting grid, the backpack's, the dispenser, the crafters of the game and of Quark) takes the crafting table's frame.
- **Arrows.** GUI Revision's wide arrows and small triangles, and vanilla's wide arrow copied by mods, become one slim engraved arrow; the furnaces' progress arrow (and the copies of it in Overgeared, EMI…) is the same arrow in the Questlog's lantern light. Arrows mods draw in shapes of their own (Envelope, Exposure, Farm & Charm, Kaleidoscope Cookery's straight and bent ones) are found by shape, whatever their colour, and redrawn the same way, at 17x8 where the mod's progress sprite is that small; Exposure's plus, the anvil's and the cartography table's become engraved too. The brewing stand's arrow is the same one turned down, empty on the panel and lit in its progress sprite.
- **Details.** Every large panel, vanilla or mod, takes the crafting table's decoration: GUI Revision's corner ornament in its four corners and two curls from the middle of Hollow GUI's frieze hanging from the top edge; an ornament that would meet a slot or a drawing is not placed, so none is ever cut, and on mod screens the top-left corner is left to the title the mod writes there. Every panel has the same frame: a moss outline, a bone light on top, a sage bevel on the sides (the enchanting table's double frame and its own corner marks are replaced). Every large panel is covered by one small leaf pattern on a single grid shared by all textures (every 10 pixels across, 8 down, staggered), one shade from the panel; it runs on between and under the slots, so it is the same on every screen whatever the layout. Where a large well touches a slot (the player in the creative inventory, the horse), the well gives way by a pixel; cells a compat drew slightly differently inside a grid (Farm & Charm, the creative inventory's top row) are found from the grid's pitch and redrawn like the others.
- **HUD.** The hotbar is redrawn as a mist bar with rounded ends and a soft shadow, its slots like those of the screens; the selection and the offhand slot match it, the selection in the Questlog's lantern light. Without the XP bar, the `fallow` mod (0.4.1, `xp` module) lowers hearts, armor, hunger, air and thirst by 5 pixels, and Cold Sweat's body icon follows through its client config ([mod-fallow](mod-fallow.md)).
- **Widgets.** `scripts/interface/widgets.py` draws the buttons, sliders, checkboxes, tabs, text fields, scrollers, small square buttons and the recipe book's toggles anew. Raised parts are sage plaques with a moss outline, rounded corners, a bone light on top, a shadow below and an engraved inner frame; recessed parts (the slider's track, checkboxes, text fields) are moss wells shaded like the slots. What the pointer is on glows in lantern light (outline and inner frame), a disabled button is darker and flat. The checkboxes are nine-sliced so that vanilla's 17-pixel box keeps its tick whole.
- **Sprites.** Toasts, the advancements' boxes and frames, the recipe book, the server and world lists and the containers' sprites take Fallow's tones by brightness; what GUI Revision leaves purple, wood or blue (the enchanting table's choices, the loom's patterns, the beacon's buttons) is brought to them too, and highlights glow in lantern light. Colours that carry a meaning (red crosses, green ticks, pings, boss bars) stay. Vanilla textures GUI Revision does not cover are taken from the game's jar and dressed alike: the crafter's screen (its grid framed like the crafting table's), text fields, tabs, checkboxes, scrollers, the bundle's and the crafter's sprites, the popup.
- **Menus.** The main menu, the options and the other menus share the same tones: the shading laid over their background, the darker band behind their lists and the separators under their header and above their footer are moss and bone instead of black and white, in game too; the statistics' sprites and the demo's panel are dressed like the rest. The title screen's panorama is a pine forest fading into the mist under a veiled sun, drawn by the script (four rows of pines, the nearest with two clearings), in place of vanilla's trial chamber.
- **Title and loading screens.** FancyMenu and Drippy Loading Screen (client only) carry two layouts written by `scripts/interface/menus.py` into `pack/config/fancymenu/`: on the title screen, the Fallow logo (stone letters in mist and moss, overgrown with grass and vines) in place of Minecraft's and no splash text, the vanilla buttons, the panorama and the required texts kept; on the loading screen, a moss ground, the logo and a bar in lantern light instead of Mojang's red screen. FancyMenu runs in modpack mode: players never see its editor. NeoForge's early loading window, before the mods load, keeps its own look.
- **Titles.** GUI Revision hides the game's container titles (`container.*` and `merchant.trades` set to a blank; Fallow adds the barrel and the crafter) and draws a few titles into the textures. The blanks are kept in `fr_fr` and `en_us`, and the drawn titles are removed: the plates of the furnaces, the crafting and enchanting tables, the traders' "TRADES" banner and Supplementaries' "TRADE OFFER" box. Text drawn in a texture cannot follow the game's language. Mod screens keep the title the mod writes, and the beacon, the creative inventory and the villager's name and level still show theirs.
- **Other mods.** Screens of mods without a compat that use vanilla's greys and whose licence allows it (Envelope, Kaleidoscope Cookery, Alex's Mobs, Companions!, TreeChop) are recoloured in the same tones; a mod's own plain grey surfaces follow the same ramp. The mod's own drawings (Chipped's tool rack, the 3D blocks of Kaleidoscope's recipes, Companions!' puppet…) keep their colours: only the menu itself is reworked. Create and its addons keep their own style, as do the config libraries and the books (Patchouli, Modonomicon, Wildex).
- **Questlog.** `scripts/interface/questlog.py` draws the journal in the same light style: mist cover with sage trims and curled corners, linen pages, sage tiles, lantern light for what is selected, the search bar written « Rechercher ».

## Limits and in-game tests

1. Hidden titles: chests, the inventory and the other vanilla containers show no title, as in GUI Revision, so the furnace, the blast furnace and the smoker look alike, as do the dispenser and the dropper, and the chests; a renamed chest still shows its name, over the top-left ornament. Only `fr_fr` and `en_us` are blanked (a `fr_ca` player sees the titles).
2. Check the hotbar, hearts and selected slot at GUI scales 2, 3 and 4, and the Questlog at its default and custom panel sizes (the nine-slice edges are stretched).
3. Check the screens of Overgeared, Cold Sweat and Supplementaries (the traders' offer bar), whose compats target the latest versions of those mods; compat files for other versions (old JEI, Immersive Enchanting, Curios and Supplementaries textures) are not shipped, so JEI keeps its own look (its lists are hidden).
4. Companions! draws English words into its card game textures (Win, Lost, Card, Stop); they come from the mod itself.
5. Thirst Was Reclaimed and Cold Sweat draw their own HUD icons; to be matched later.
6. The FancyMenu and Drippy layouts are written by hand, never opened in their editor: check that the title screen shows the Fallow logo without the Minecraft logo or splash text, and that the loading screen after a resource reload (F3+T) is moss green with the logo and a lantern-coloured bar. If a layout is ignored, open it once in FancyMenu's editor (set `modpack_mode` to false in `config/fancymenu/options.txt`) and save it.
7. Widgets: the buttons' hover in lantern light, a ticked checkbox in the options (17-pixel box), the sliders' knob, the tabs of the world creation screen.

## History

Before this pack, two drawn-from-scratch iterations (2026-10-19, then two on 2026-10-26: leather and parchment, then moss slate) were found not clean enough; the maintainer then asked to start from a free-licensed pack, and picked the GUI Revision + Hollow GUI mix. A first review then asked for a regular background pattern instead of scattered details, rounder slots, and every screen in harmony: hence the leaf pattern, the rounded slots and the greyed screens. A second review (same day) asked for the polish of Dark Coffee GUI without its pixels: no names drawn in textures, slimmer arrows, no light line over the slots, a tighter pattern at the same spacing everywhere, cast shadows, rounded slots, a textured well behind the player, a hotbar with rounded ends, and mods' own drawings left untouched. A third review asked for no dark line over the slots, rounded squares instead of round slots, and a finer well behind the player than the grid: the slots now take Dark Coffee's construction (outline, inner shadow, corner profile) redrawn in Fallow's colours, and the well its fine grain. A fourth review asked that nothing be cut or nearly overlap, and that the bars come down into the space of the XP bar. A fifth review asked for the crafting table's corners and curls on every screen, one leaf pattern that does not move with the slots, and a full grain behind the player without the shadow at its top. A sixth review asked for the mods' arrows, the stray light marks around the player, straight crosses between slots, and the crafting table's frame on the other crafting grids. A last full audit before release (vanilla screens, sprites, every mod screen, code and packaging) then fixed the sprites left in GUI Revision's greys and colours, the brewing stand's arrow, the enchanting table's frame, the hopper's open edge, leftovers of old arrows and wells in mod screens, the textures no version reads, the licences of recoloured mod textures, and added the crafter's screen and a pack icon. The menus were then brought to the same tones (shading, separators, a misty forest panorama), the widgets were redrawn (the hover state was darker than the rest state, and the lists' arrows turned green), and FancyMenu and Drippy were added for a Fallow title and loading screen.
