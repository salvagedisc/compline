# Maximum health: lose it by dying, regain it by exploring

Idea 3 drawn from Wayfarer ([research of 2026-10-19](../archive/plan/recherche/2026-10-19/wayfarer.md), recommendation 3). Death costs something without adding combat, and to make up for the loss you have to go back out exploring.

## The rule, for a player

- You start with 10 hearts.
- Each death removes 1 heart of maximum health. On waking, a message tells you how many you have left.
- You never drop below 5 hearts.
- A **Life Shard** (`fallow:eclat_de_vie`) gives you back 1 heart and fills it. A right click is enough. You never exceed 10 hearts: with full health, the shard refuses to be used and says so.
- Shards are found by exploring: in vanilla dungeons, temples and shipwrecks, in ocean ruins and archaeology sites, and near the Elderhearts of the monuments. No creature drops them, and no recipe makes them.
- The shard's tooltip shows your current hearts (for example "Cœurs : 7 / 10", i.e. "Hearts: 7 / 10").
- Your lost hearts stay lost after a disconnection or a server restart.
- Your items are not lost: since 2026-11-18 they stay in the body you leave behind, until you come back for it ([0037](../decisions/0037-ragdoll-corpse.md)).

## Technical operation

- **State**: the number of lost hearts is an integer `fallow_sante_perdus` in the player's KubeJS persistent data. It is saved with the player (NBT key `KubeJSPersistentData`), and KubeJS copies it into the new player at each respawn.
- **Effect**: a single `fallow:mort` modifier on the `minecraft:generic.max_health` attribute, of type `add_value`, worth −2 × lost hearts. It is transient: the script reapplies it at login (`PlayerEvents.loggedIn`), at cloning (`PlayerEvents.cloned`) and at respawn (`PlayerEvents.respawned`). In 1.21.1, a death only copies the base values of attributes (`assignBaseValues`), so the modifiers disappear. They must be reapplied.
- **Death**: it is counted in `PlayerEvents.cloned` when `keepData` is false, that is, when NeoForge reports `wasDeath`. A totem of undying prevents death, so there is no loss.
- **Shard**: `ItemEvents.rightClicked`. It gives back 1 heart and heals 2 points. The item has a one-second cooldown. The player makes an arm swing, hears the vanilla sound `block.amethyst_block.chime` and sees heart particles. In creative, the shard is not consumed.
- **Elderheart**: the Ancient Remnants monuments have **no chest**. The structure templates in the 1.5.2 jar contain only spawners, and AR has no chest loot table. The reward therefore comes from the `ancient_remnants:elderheart` entity: every 5 seconds, a player within 16 blocks of an Elderheart they have never approached receives a shard. This is the radius in which AR gives its blessing. The script remembers the UUID of each Elderheart in `fallow_sante_coeurs_anciens`, so a monument gives one shard per player, once.

## Sources and rates

A rate is the chance that **one roll** of the table gives a shard. A roll is an opened chest, a broken pot or a brushed suspicious block. With Lootr, each player has their own roll for each chest.

### Chests (the shard is added to the loot)

Since 2026-11-13 all the tables are vanilla ones: No Man's Land, whose tables were used before, left the pack ([0019](../decisions/0019-leaving-no-mans-land.md)). The rates below keep the same order of magnitude as the old ones. **They are provisional paper values, to be confirmed by the team.**

| Table | Where | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| `minecraft:chests/simple_dungeon` | Dungeons (monster rooms) | 10% |
| `minecraft:chests/jungle_temple` | Jungle temple | 25% |
| `minecraft:chests/desert_pyramid` | Desert pyramid | 5% |
| `minecraft:chests/shipwreck_treasure` | Shipwreck treasure chest | 15% |
| `minecraft:chests/shipwreck_supply` | Shipwreck supply chest | 5% |
| `minecraft:chests/underwater_ruin_big` | Large ocean ruins | 12% |
| `minecraft:chests/underwater_ruin_small` | Small ocean ruins | 5% |

### Archaeology (the shard replaces the find)

A suspicious block only yields one item: when the shard comes out, it replaces the planned item.

| Table | Where | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| `minecraft:archaeology/trail_ruins_rare` | Rare finds of trail ruins | 3% |
| `minecraft:archaeology/desert_well` | Suspicious sand of desert wells | 5% |
| `minecraft:archaeology/ocean_ruin_cold` | Cold ocean ruins | 4% |
| `minecraft:archaeology/ocean_ruin_warm` | Warm ocean ruins | 4% |

### Elderhearts (Ancient Remnants)

There is one per monument (6 types, spacing 100/20): 1 shard on each player's first visit.

### Removed on 2026-11-13

The No Man's Land tables (small dungeon cores, deep wells, sniffer monument, alchemist pots, and the archaeology of alchemist ruins, wells and sniffer monument) went with the mod. Before that, `minecraft:chests/simple_dungeon` and `minecraft:archaeology/desert_well` had been left out because No Man's Land used them very often; with vanilla structures they are back.

### Target order of magnitude

The aim is a shard **every 2 to 3 hours** of regular exploration. That estimate was made with the No Man's Land sources (small dungeons, wells and alchemist ruins, which it made frequent). **It has not been recomputed** for the vanilla sources above, whose frequency in the new world is not known: **to be recomputed**, then the rates adjusted, ideally from a count in game.

## Tuning the numbers

All numbers are named constants at the top of the file.

- `server_scripts/sante.js`:
  - `SANTE_COEURS_DEPART` (10), `SANTE_COEURS_PLANCHER` (5);
  - `SANTE_COEURS_PERDUS_PAR_MORT` (1), `SANTE_COEURS_RENDUS_PAR_ECLAT` (1);
  - `SANTE_SOIN_A_L_USAGE` (2 points), `SANTE_DELAI_ECLAT` (20 ticks);
  - `SANTE_COEUR_ANCIEN_ECLATS` (1), `SANTE_COEUR_ANCIEN_RAYON` (16), `SANTE_COEUR_ANCIEN_INTERVALLE` (100 ticks).
- `server_scripts/sante_butin.js`: the `SANTE_BUTIN_COFFRES` and `SANTE_BUTIN_ARCHEOLOGIE` objects, in the form table → chance (from 0 to 1). To disable a source, remove its line.
- `client_scripts/sante.js`: `SANTE_CLIENT_COEURS_DEPART` must stay equal to `SANTE_COEURS_DEPART`.
- After a change: `/reload` for server scripts and loot, F3+T for the tooltip. The startup script (the item) requires a restart.
- **Do not rename** `fallow:mort` or the `fallow_sante_*` keys on a running world: players would lose their state.

## Compatibility with other sources of maximum health

`MAX_HEALTH` and `max_health` were searched for in all the jars of the pack.

- **Ancient Remnants**: no blessing touches maximum health. "Desperate Vitality" gives Regeneration below 33% health, and the Sentinel's Aegis gives armor.
- **Cold Sweat, Overgeared, Kaleidoscope Cookery (replaced by Farmer's Delight on 2026-11-15, not re-read), Thirst Was Reclaimed**: nothing on the player's maximum health. Cold Sweat only uses it for its chameleon.
- **Traveler's Backpack**: the Warden backpack adds +4 with its own identifier `travelersbackpack:warden_backpack_max_health`. The two modifiers add up.
- **Vanilla Health Boost effect**: it also has its own modifier, and they add up.
- The script only reads and modifies `fallow:mort`. The cap of 10 hearts applies only to the shard: a bonus from another source can still exceed 10.

## Verified on the test server (2026-10-19, no player)

- 0 KubeJS errors: 2/2 startup scripts, 5/5 server scripts. `fallow:eclat_de_vie` is registered: `/summon item` returns `item.fallow.eclat_de_vie`.
- Loot, shards obtained with `/loot spawn` (2026-10-19, with the No Man's Land tables of the time; the vanilla rates above have not been run): `shipwreck_treasure` 10/60 then 10/40, `small_dungeon_core` 8/100, `water_well` 10/60, `sniffer_monument` 19/60, `alchemist_pot_treasure` 3/200, `shipwreck_supply` 10/120, `underwater_ruin_big` 13/100, `underwater_ruin_small` 4/120. The actual rates are close to the planned rates.
- Archaeology: at most one item per roll (197/200, 149/150, 120/120, 149/150 and 150/150 entities), with 2 to 7% shards. No "too many items" warning.
- APIs tried on a pig:
  - `modifyAttribute(..., 'add_value')` with strings works. Reapplying it replaces the old modifier, and `removeAttribute` removes it. `getAttribute(...).getModifier('fallow:mort').amount()` reads the value correctly.
  - `persistentData`: `getInt`, `putInt`, `getBoolean`, `getString` and `remove` work.
  - `getEntitiesWithin(AABB.of(...))` and `.type` work. They see a summoned `ancient_remnants:elderheart`.
  - Health is **not** automatically brought down to the new maximum: the script does it itself.
- Two Rhino pitfalls, fixed in the scripts:
  - `const` in a `try` block raises "redeclaration of var": the client uses `let`;
  - `entity.distanceTo(other)` is ambiguous: the distance is computed by hand.
- Not verifiable without a player: death, waking, the right-click shard (`cooldowns`, `swing`, `heal`), the Elderheart reward in `PlayerEvents.tick`, the tooltip and the tint (client side), and pots or chests of the vanilla structures opened in game.
