# Research: Compline zoo module (the keeper's register)

Date: 2026-10-26. Research report. Everything cited comes from pages that were really opened (list at the end of the document). When a piece of information comes only from a search engine snippet, this is stated.

## 0. Limits of the research

- Several sites are closed to the tools (Bulbapedia, Fandom, Moddex, official Planet Zoo, Reddit, X). So I have no player feedback on Menagerie, except one Moddex review seen in a search summary.
- The Menagerie CurseForge page gives the mod list, but almost nothing on how the quests work. The details come from the release notes.
- I could not confirm that Mowzie's Mobs is in Menagerie. It does not appear in the list I read.

## 1. The "Menagerie" modpack

### Which pack?

There are three, and they must not be confused.

- **Menagerie - a zoo modpack**, by jamono. Forge 1.20.1, about 255 mods, about 140,000 downloads. Described as "Light-questing, building and exploration pack about building a ZOO". This is the right one. Categories: exploration, vanilla+, quests.
- **MENAGERIE - Paradoxical Wilds**: another pack, centered on the Marvelous Menagerie mod. No zoo, no quests. It sells the mood of "slightly wrong" creatures in disturbing biomes.
- **Zookeeper's Edition**: a Menagerie addon with quests and species, noted as abandoned (secondary source).

### What is inside (CurseForge page)

- Quests: FTB Quests, FTB Teams, FTB Library, a quest book resource pack.
- Animals: Alex's Mobs, Critters and Companions, Naturalist, Bountiful Critters, Domestication Innovation, Odd Organisms, Faunify, Ecologics, Recrafted Creatures, Spawnt, Ben's Sharks and others.
- Larger creatures: Alex's Caves, Peculiar Primordials, Unusual Prehistory, Rainbow Reef. So yes, the pack goes beyond ordinary animals: prehistoric, abyssal and cave creatures.
- Building: Architect's Palette, FramedBlocks, Supplementaries, Farmer's Delight.

So the maintainer is right about the broader scope. It is not confirmed that Mowzie's-type bosses are in it.

### How completion works

From the 2.0 and 2.2 release notes (CurseForge):

- **One enclosure quest per creature.** It gives "research papers", more of them for harder creatures.
- **"Challenge" section** for dangerous fauna (predator arenas).
- **"Safari Rescuers" section**: papers are traded for mob crates, then a random crate (2.2). This is the engine for obtaining animals.
- **"Discovery" section**: information quests, a "Photograph Frame" quest, added descriptions, a tip about forming a group with friends.
- Rewards as pictures: "Anomalocaris picture reward", so decorative paintings to collect.
- Botany and Artifacts sections added in 2.2.
- For Odd Organisms, the creature becomes available when its DNA is "acquirable": there is a DNA logic.
- A secondary source says that finishing an enclosure is ticked by the player (manual validation).

### How animals are found

The pages do not say. We only see that animals appear through the creature mods, and that the quest points toward new species and "enhanced biomes". There is also a "Structure Compass" item added in 2.2. So the search is not guided by precise clues.

### Verdict

- Good: a reward (papers) tied to the **difficulty** of the beast, not to a simple count. A trade that turns research into currency, with some chance (random crate).
- Good: collectible pictures as a reward.
- Weak: a summarized Moddex review says "cozy" but "did not make me want to build infrastructure". The manual validation of enclosures and the weak link between enclosure quality and reward are a lesson: a zoo that measures nothing does not motivate.

## 2. Other Minecraft references

### Alex's Mobs: Animal Dictionary

- A built-in book that documents each creature: 3D preview, spawn biome, diet, taming or breeding items, drops. Source: the 9minecraft wiki, which stays vague on how to obtain them.
- Good: a useful sheet (biome + food), given early.
- To avoid: knowing everything from the start removes the investigation. For Compline, the sheet must fill in through observation.
- To take: the biome + food pair is exactly what the register must display.

### Cobblemon: Pokédex, Cobbledex, Research Tasks

- **Cobbledex**: rewards based on the number of different species caught (forms counted separately). The "where to find" feature can be made to depend on capture (`HowToFind_NeedCatch`). Server-side config only.
- **Cobblemon: Research Tasks**: 5 to 8 tasks per species (evolve, defeat, friendship 100, fish, ride over 3000 blocks...), 7 tiers of total completion in a research book, all configurable in JSON. Author's note: some tasks are inflated because they are generated by category.
- Good: several tasks per species, adapted to its particularities (no fishing task if the species cannot be fished).
- To avoid: mass-generated tasks, inflated numbers, 6000 tasks. The grind.

### Wildex Bestiary (already in the pack)

- Discovery by kill, spyglass, **photography through Exposure**, sharing between players, and an Analyzer block on a drop. Tracked per player and per world.
- Completion reward: the "Spyglass Pulse" (highlights mobs through walls, with a delay).
- **Hooks for our module**: Java API `WildexApi` with `getDiscoveredCount`, `getTotalCount`, `getCompletionPercentScaled`, `isComplete`. Events `WildexDiscoveryChangedEvent` and `WildexCompletedEvent`. KubeJS bridge (`wildex.discovery_changed`, `wildex.completed`). Scoreboard objectives `wildex_discovered`, `wildex_total`, `wildex_percent`, `wildex_complete`. Config `hiddenMode`, `excludedModIds`, `exposureDiscoveryEnabled`, `spyglassDiscoveryChargeTicks`.
- Limit: the API is global (count, percentage). The README does not document a per-entity query from another mod. The register will either have to listen to the event (field `mobId`), or keep its own list. To check in the source code or by decompilation before committing to it.
- The 3.0.0 entries keep discovery details, but earlier ones do not.

### "Zoo" packs and captures

- **Zeno's Zoo Craft**: breeding, exhibits, finances. Very vague page, nothing instructive on the substance.
- Capture mods for 1.21.1 NeoForge: Caged Mobs Reborn (DNA sample and cages), Better Cage Trap, Mob Catcher (net). They move beasts around, which Compline does not want for rare creatures. For common fauna, a cage or a closed enclosure is enough.
- Naturalist: 24 to 47 animals depending on the page, behaviors and drops. No observation tool.

## 3. Outside Minecraft: the six most instructive

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus (research tasks)

- Each species has its own tasks (catch N, defeat, see a move, feed, see it jumping from a tree, forms, etc.). Each completed task gives +1 level, some +2. Level 10 = complete entry.
- Tasks count even without capture, but the entry only appears on capture.
- Good: the observation of **behaviors** is rewarded, not only the catch. The player looks at the beast up close.
- To avoid: counting tasks ("catch 20") and the mandatory report to the professor.
- To take: research levels per species, behavior tasks.

### Planet Zoo (welfare)

- Four needs: nutrition, social, habitat, enrichment (from a search snippet of the official guide, page blocked; the open third-party guide covers food, space, terrain and plants, social, enrichment). Habitat groups cleanliness, temperature, terrain, plants, shelters. Each species has its own preferred terrain split.
- Each animal has a panel that lists its needs by category, with "below desired level" or "dropping" in red. Size counts in **usable** space. Group size is specific to the species, shelters for shy ones.
- Good: the game says **why** things go badly. You fix the red category.
- To avoid: a dozen gauges, too heavy for a Minecraft block.
- To take: 3 or 4 readable criteria, with the cause shown.

### Monster Hunter World (field guide)

- Tracks (footprints, etc.) that are picked up feed the monster's entry and the tracking level. The entry gets more precise in tiers. Scoutflies give more help when you have research, and this level **decays over time** (Fextralife page). According to unopened search snippets: 4 to 6 research levels per monster, with weak points and drops added.
- Good: a **field** clue (tracks) that leads to the beast, with no coordinate map.
- To take: tracks and material clues as a search aid, which run out.

### Red Dead Redemption 2 (compendium)

- 178 animals. States: studied (binoculars, button hold), hunted, skinned. The Zoologist achievement only requires study, **without killing**. Legendary animals and a few missable animals are excluded from the count. Spawn tips: some at night, others by day, retry by sleeping.
- Good: looking is enough. The "good" observation game is non-destructive. Missable creatures do not block 100%.
- To avoid: hunting the 4 or 5 missing animals, with no clue, through save reloads.
- To take: "studied" as a tier of observation from a distance, and exclude missable cases from the total.

### Animal Crossing: the museum

- The museum must be built before collecting. Each room reflects its contents (fossils in the basement, fish in dark tanks, insects in habitat). Blathers tells a fact about each donation. The camera moves closer when you stop. Empty display cases suggest what remains. Donations advance the visible transformation of the place.
- Good: progress is **visible in space**, and calm.
- To take: the milestone changes the place (the clearing changes), and the gaps are an invitation, not a list.

### Ark: the dossiers

- Creature dossiers are found on the map where the creature spawns. They describe behavior, habits, taming food. Boss dossiers can only be obtained after the boss. Global completion reward: extra levels. Originally, they were earned by killing or taming.
- Good: knowledge is **in the world**, to be found.
- To avoid: scattered notes that become a collecting chore.

Subnautica (scan database) was not kept: I only found guides for the second game.

## 4. Recommendations for the zoo module

1. **Research levels per species, not just "seen".** Five tiers: Aperçu (Glimpsed), Observé (Observed, spyglass or photo), Étudié (Studied, time spent near the animal), Hébergé (Housed, ordinary fauna only), Compris (Understood, behavior seen, for example eating, sleeping or moving in a group). Each tier fills one line of the register. Tasks are few, 3 to 5 per species, and adapted (never a "fish" task for a land animal).
2. **Two paths, not one.** Ordinary fauna: it is housed in the closed enclosure, as planned. Rare creatures and Mowzie's beings: they are never enclosed. They count through **observation and photography** (Exposure, spyglass). This avoids breaking immersion and rarity.
3. **A single count of "species" that mixes both paths** for milestones 5, 15, 30, 50. A rare being is worth more than a common species (for example 3 points), like Menagerie, which pays by difficulty.
4. **Readable habitat score, 4 criteria maximum**: usable space (free volume), terrain (blocks the species likes, via data tags), shelter (at least one covered block), reachable food. The register shows one line per criterion with the cause ("terrain : il manque de la mousse", meaning "terrain: moss is missing"), Planet Zoo style. No hidden gauge.
5. **Social and enrichment: one single setting.** Desired group size per species (tag or data). No boredom, dirt or finance gauge. Out of scope.
6. **The register does not validate by hand.** Unlike Menagerie, it measures. No "done" click.
7. **Clues without a compass.** When a species is known at the Aperçu tier, the register writes a vague clue in French: habitat, time, sign ("des empreintes près de l'eau claire", meaning "footprints near clear water"). The **tracks** (feathers, footprints, nest) are rare items or blocks in the world that raise the clue level, and that **fade over time** (Monster Hunter idea). No coordinates, no map.
8. **A sheet that fills in.** Biome and food are only shown after observation. Do not offer a complete dictionary up front (Alex's Mobs lesson).
9. **Wildex link.** Condition: the species is already in the Wildex to appear as "known". Listen to `WildexDiscoveryChangedEvent` (field `mobId`) and read `WildexApi`. Check in the Wildex code whether a specific entity can be queried. If not, keep our own list fed by the event. Use `excludedModIds` to leave out technical mobs.
10. **Exposure link.** A photo that contains the animal counts as the "Observé" tier. The Wildex already grants progress through photo frames (option `exposureDiscoveryEnabled`). Our module can show the photo in the register page or in a frame on the enclosure wall. For rare creatures, **a photo is the only proof** of an encounter, which gives the quest a purpose.
11. **Zoo = sum of the registers of one clearing.** Duplicate species count only once. An extra register earns nothing if it adds no species. This prevents register abuse.
12. **Rewards that change the place, not items.** 5 species: the clearing grows a little. 15: soft music per biome (like the museum pieces in Animal Crossing). 30: a place to build (monument), with "empty display cases" that show what is missing. 50: final monument. No papers or currency: the Menagerie quest currency does not fit an almost empty world.
13. **Rare beings are never counted as missable.** Exclude from the total anything that can disappear (killed bosses, one-time events) or that can have an "Observé" tier that is enough. RDR2 lesson: no 100% blocked by a single missed beast.
14. **A short story per tier, in French.** One or two sentences from the keeper at each tier (like Blathers and the Ark dossiers), in a style that stays enigmatic, without horror.
15. **To leave out:** captures of rare creatures (Cage Trap, Mob Catcher), finances and visitors, hundreds of generated tasks (Research Tasks), trades and random crates, manual validation, upkeep gauges (cleanliness, disease), rankings between players. The zoo is a shared project among friends, not an economy.

### Open points to decide

- Does the Wildex API allow a per-entity query? If not, our event listening will be enough.
- Which Mowzie's and Alex's Mobs mobs are "to house" or "to observe only"? A list in data tags, by category, has to be written.
- Exact weight of rare beings in milestones 30 and 50.
