# Fallow — Vision

## Pitch
A vast, damp, almost empty world, in the spirit of the No Man's Land trailer: landscapes in the mist, ruins, menhirs, a lone traveller. A handful of players arrive, settle in, then slowly push back the frontier: cabin, farm, hamlet, railway, factory, airfield, distant colonies. Wherever they bring life back, the mist lifts and the distant landscape appears. There is no end: the permanent world is for perfecting one's installations.

## Art direction
- Mist, distance, silhouettes: the world is hard to read from afar.
- Solitude and a lingering strangeness, **enigmatic, never dark**: no horror, no jumpscares, no sanity gauge.
- **The mist lifts**: in places, at certain moments, and above all in the clearings the players create. There the grass turns vivid, the music changes and the far view opens (Distant Horizons).
- The base is an island of light; moving away from it means losing one's bearings.
- No shaders and no realistic textures (Create Aeronautics has known problems with Iris): the atmosphere comes from the terrain, the mist, coloured light, particles, sounds and music.
- **A polished, connected finish**, in the manner of Realism Craft: travelling, building, eating, cutting a tree or opening one's bag must be pleasant to look at and to perform, and the systems talk to each other.

## Three pillars
1. **Exploration** — "I wonder what's over there", not "I need X diamonds". Inspiration: Zelda Breath of the Wild. No coordinates: the map builds itself (Antique Atlas). Monuments, the Friendly Moon and lost letters give the world its enigmas.
2. **Civilisation** — Forging (Overgeared) → workshop → industry (Create) → rail (Steam 'n' Rails) → aviation (Aeronautics). Each step widens the land one can clear of mist.
3. **Daily life** — Water → farming → animal husbandry → cooking (Kaleidoscope Cookery) → tannery → expedition.

## Long-term goals
A zoo and its keeper's ledger, clearings joined into a living territory, a rail and air network, the monuments' codex, a complete atlas, photographs of species and stars.

## Survival
Harder because of the environment (temperature, altitude, thirst, seasons), not because of combat. Few hostile creatures: rare, territorial and polished (quality models and animations). No vanilla monsters. No experience: tools wear out and knowledge is found. Each death costs a heart, down to five.

## Prohibitions
- Minimap, waypoints, teleportation, compasses that point to structures.
- Hostile vanilla monsters, difficulty through hordes.
- End and Nether closed, no Elytra ([decision 0004](decisions/0004-structures.md)).
- Piling up Create addons or overlapping food mods.

## For the players
Everything that can be read in game (mechanics, books, quests, codex, bestiary, tooltips) is in French: one of the players does not speak English. Every mod added must be translated ([translation](plan/traduction.md)).

## Working rules
- Every suggestion is a hypothesis: we compare for real before deciding.
- The 5-question filter for every mod: does it change the gameplay? does it fit with the other systems? is it still interesting after 100 hours? does it bring a mechanic rather than content? does it avoid overlaps?
- Then the whole pack is judged on four more: **weight** (does it change what the player decides or feels?), **tone** (does it keep the world almost empty and enigmatic?), **link** (does it plug into wood, temperature, cooking, fauna, the map?), **cost** (stable and light for a world meant to last years?) ([10.2](plan/10-recentrage.md#102-the-basic-idea)).
- "Add 200 items" is not an argument; "turns an activity into a craft" is.
- What scripts cannot do goes into the custom `fallow` mod, one module per feature, each with its own switch ([mod-fallow](plan/mod-fallow.md)).
